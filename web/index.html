SKYLYNNE

An ambient world that plays itself.

Six voices on one tempo and one scale, shaped live with four knobs, over a quiet cliff at dusk. Bells like ice, pads that drift, a deep slow sub. For the M-VAVE FM-1.

Try it in your browser

Installing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. On this device you can still try SKYLYNNE with sound.

The FM-1 restarted with SKYLYNNE. You can unplug it now. Turn MASTER up slowly: the first minute is quiet on purpose.

The install stopped. In most cases you can start it again.

Stopped before “Switching to update mode”? Nothing was written. Plug the cable back in and press Install again.

Stopped while writing, with the FM-1 still on? It waits in update mode with a black screen. Plug the cable back in and press Install again. Writing starts again from the beginning.

Did it switch off? Hold OCT− while switching on: update mode. Press Install again.

No flash at all, or a USB disk called WL82 UBOOT1.00? See “Something went wrong” below.

The world

Six voices, one tempo, one scale

There is no pattern to program. Every voice plays by its own rhythm against the others, so the music drifts and never repeats. You shape it while it plays: how much happens, how far it wanders, how bright it is, how far away it sounds.

Voices

Each of the six is a resonator, a plucked string, an FM bell, a pad or a breath of air. Change a voice’s kind, level, pan, decay and material at any time.

Biomes

A biome is a whole soundscape: scale, tempo, room and six voices. Velvet Mist, Harbour Fog, Snowlight, Nightdrive, Ice Garden, Tide, Tin Roof, Afterglow.

Space

A huge reverb, a tempo-synced ping-pong delay and a granular cloud turn each note into a place. The whole room is one knob.

Controls

Four knobs and a few gestures

K1 – K4Density, motion, colour, space
SELECTChoose a voice. The figure on the cliff points at it.
ALGORITHMChange that voice’s kind
PRESETSChange biome
EDITThe voice page: level, pan, decay, material
OCT− / OCT+Tempo
PLAYHold: the voices wait and the sound rings out
Keys 1 – 6Mute a voice
Other keysPlay the selected voice on the biome’s scale

Physics sequencer

A shape turns. Balls fall. Walls play notes.

Press SEQ. A polygon spins, balls fall inside it, and every ball that touches a side plays a note: the side is the scale degree, the ball is a voice, the speed of the hit is how hard. Spin, gravity, bounce and the number of balls are the four knobs; PRESETS changes the number of sides.

Install

Your FM-1, in about a minute

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, plug the FM-1 in with a data cable and press Install above. Over the official firmware or any firmware that answers, it switches itself to update mode. Already on SKYLYNNE? Hold OCT−, OCT+ and HOME for five seconds (or hold OCT− while switching on) to put it in update mode, then press Install.

        Something went wrong
        
An FM-1 that shows nothing and does not answer can still be written: hold OCT− while switching on. If a USB disk called “WL82 UBOOT1.00” appears, the chip’s own boot loader is running; the community unbricker at Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker restores it.
