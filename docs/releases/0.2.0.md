SKYLYNNE 0.2.0: a new, standalone firmware.

- Six self-playing voices on one tempo and scale (resonator, pluck, FM bell, pad, air), each editable.
- Four macros: DENSITY, MOTION, COLOUR, SPACE. Biomes as presets.
- A physics sequencer: a spinning polygon with falling balls; wall hits play notes.
- A dusk-and-night cliff scene with a reflection, fireflies and a figure who reacts to the knobs.
- Not yet: USB MIDI out for the physics sequencer, saving user biomes.
